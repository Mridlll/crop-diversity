Effective number of crops in each district, year by year

Districts move from year to year while the national spread of land across crops stays flat.

Weighted by cropped area, the national effective number of crops stays flat from 1998–99 to 2019–20. District by district, 297 ended the period with a lower effective number of crops and 203 with a higher one, although the median change is only about a sixth of a crop.

Effective number of crops, by year

Drag the slider or press Play. Brighter means a higher effective number of crops, on the same scale every year.

Districts with no record that year are unshaded. The record starts in 1998–99 because the 1997–98 returns are broken, with whole seasons missing in some states.

Why the early years show fewer districts

Because districts enter the record when they are created, the early years show fewer of them. National figures use only the districts that report in every year, which keeps new districts from passing for change on the land.

Which way districts moved

Between 1998–2004 and 2013–2019 the median district lost 0.17 of an effective crop. With districts weighted by their cropped land the fall is 0.20, which is not statistically significant once each state's swings in reporting are allowed for.
