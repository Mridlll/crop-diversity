Where the measures disagree

The count of crops gives a crop on one hectare the same weight as the staple, whereas the effective number of crops and the number of main crops weigh each crop by the land it covers. Chhattisgarh and Assam grow many crops, yet rice takes so much of their land that they drop near the bottom on the effective number.

Karnataka, interior Andhra Pradesh, Madhya Pradesh and Rajasthan stay near the top on the count of crops, the effective number of crops and the number of main crops, although their evenness, the effective number divided by the count, is no better than middling. A district can grow many crops and still spread its land unevenly across them.
