Share of each district’s cropped area under food crops

The share of each district’s cropped land under crops that people eat.

In five of every six districts, food crops cover at least 90 percent of the cropped area. The districts with the lowest shares are mostly cotton districts in Telangana, Gujarat and Maharashtra.

Share of cropped area under food crops

Brighter means a larger share under food crops. Hover over a district for its largest crops.

Food crops are cereals, pulses, oilseeds, fruit, vegetables, sugar and spices. The remaining land is under fibre crops, fodder crops, and drugs and narcotics.

Where the land is not growing food

The food share is lowest in the cotton belt across Gujarat, Maharashtra and Telangana, and in the guar districts of western Rajasthan. Because cotton tends to share a district's land with several other crops, 42 of the 64 districts with less than 80 percent of their land under food crops have an effective number of crops above the median district.

A crop counts as food by its category, whatever later happens to the harvest, so maize counts as food even when it is grown for poultry feed.
