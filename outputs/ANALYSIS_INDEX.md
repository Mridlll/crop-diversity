# Crop-diversity repository: analysis index

Written 7 November 2026 against repository HEAD 18c9278 ("Add a Neighbours page"). Purpose: one place that lists every analysis in the repo, where its numbers live, how far it survived the referee passes, and which page of the site states it, so the work can later be turned into a paper. Every number below is quoted from a file in `outputs/` (file named beside it) or from a page in `docs/`. Nothing was re-estimated for this index.

Status labels used in the register:

- **Refereed-survives**: an adversarial pass (a `*referee*` script or a `referee/` folder) re-ran it and it stood.
- **Refereed-weakened**: it stood in part. The row says what was lost.
- **Refuted**: the referee pass showed the original statement was wrong or arbitrary.
- **Superseded**: replaced by a later, better construction or a better-stated version of the same claim. The row names what replaced it.
- **Exploratory**: descriptive or unrefereed. A row marked "no referee pass" has not been attacked, only reproduced.

Pages are `docs/*.html`: Overview (`index`), Measurement, Irrigation, Markets, Crop groups, Neighbours, Data and methods (`data`), and the map pages Timeline, Diversity, Calorie-diversity, Food-nonfood, Gallery.

## 2. Conventions

### Year rule
- One place sets the window: `scripts/_years.py`, `FIRST_YEAR = 1998` (1998-99) to `LAST_YEAR = 2019` (2019-20), 22 years. Every multi-year district average and every change over time uses `keep_years()`.
- 1997-98 is dropped because the raw rows are broken, not because anything changed on the ground (`outputs/revision_nov8/d0_results.md`, section 2): West Bengal files 50.8 Mha of rabi niger seed (26 rows; 28 percent of all area recorded that year; 7,587 ha in 1998-99), Odisha has no kharif (0 against 5.16 Mha; whole state 0.91 Mha against 5.23), Chhattisgarh kharif is 0.48 Mha against 4.56, Tamil Nadu, Himachal Pradesh and Tripura are absent, and twelve states file crop lists under 60 percent of their usual length. Dropping the West Bengal niger rows alone moves 1997-98 area-weighted D1 from 3.81 to 4.74 (1998-2004 mean 5.56).
- 2020-21 is a 319-row stub (script 76, defect 1) and is dropped. The extension (script 120) replaces it with 18,599 rows for 2020-21 and adds 2021-22 and 2022-23, used only in the "recent window" irrigation work, never in the headline averages.
- Crop-group panel files still carry 1997-98; the analysis scripts remove it through `_years.py`. The 725 districts in the raw cleaned file include one that exists only in 1997-98, so all post-drop work has 724 (14,089 district-years with 1997-98, 13,605 without).
- Older outputs dated before the 0f06f4e commit may still describe 24 years or 1997-98; see section 6.

### Samples
- **724** districts in the corrected crop record (725 including the 1997-98-only district). Run-all inputs and the Data page both use 724/725.
- **606** analysis districts in 30 states (funnel in `docs/data/site_data_funnel.json`: 724, matched to a 2011 district 722, have a Mission Antyodaya record 713, survey reaches at least a quarter of villages 697, at least ten years of crop data 606). Rule is in `77_final_results_corrected.py` and repeated in `95_data_page_export.py`.
- The 606 sit in **605 clusters** on 2011 parents (Sultanpur and Amethi share one). 162 of the 724 districts share a parent with another crop-record district; 64 of the 606 carry the `shared_parent` flag, and "drop shared parents" leaves 542.
- **724 national**: used for the Overview means (20.6 crops, effective number 4.73, main crops 3.34, evenness 0.260) and for the Gallery/map layers. Analysis-sample means (606): count of crops 21.4, effective number 4.9, main crops 3.5, evenness 0.250.
- **Balanced panels** for national series: 500 districts present in every year 1998-99 to 2019-20 (the site series, after dropping 1997-98); 429 present in all 23 years including 1997-98 (the older D0 note `d0_results.md`); 352 "strict balanced" units with all 22 years usable (crop groups).
- Other samples quoted in the register: 467 endpoint units (crop-group trends), 561 or 560 season-reliable districts, 600 districts with LUS irrigation, 539 with a usable 1991 irrigation record, 534 in the census source comparison (Maharashtra excluded), 598 and 591 in the recent windows.

### Hill numbers (names used on the site)
- **Count of crops, D0**: crops with positive area in the district-year.
- **Effective number of crops, D1** = exp(Shannon) of area shares. The main outcome throughout.
- **Number of main crops, D2** = 1 / sum of squared shares (inverse Simpson).
- **Evenness** = D1 / D0.
- D0 >= D1 >= D2 holds in every one of the 13,605 district-years. All are computed per district-year and then averaged over a district's years, never pooled. The thresholded count D0 at 0.1, 0.5 and 1 percent keeps crops holding at least that share of the district's gross cropped area that year (`D0_05` in file and column names).
- The composite Agro-Biodiversity Index (ABI; mean of min-max Shannon, Simpson, richness) is kept only as a comparison row. It is sample-dependent (random 80 percent subsamples move a district's score by 0.0032 on average, 0.0190 at most), equal-weighted without argument, and Shannon and Simpson correlate 0.943 (`corrected_vs_original.md`, page Measurement section 8).
- Fine point: "Whole Year" crops are summed with the named seasons into annual gross cropped area (a field cropped twice counts twice).

### Within-state comparison
- "Within state" always means state fixed effects: a Punjab district is set only against other Punjab districts. States explain 49 percent of the spread in D1 and 82 percent of the spread in D0 across the 606 (Markets page, section 7), so the pooled cross-section is read only through state fixed effects. Irrigation and its square raise R2 from 0.067 (no fixed effects) to 0.534 (with state fixed effects) (`final_results.md`).
- Panels add district fixed effects and state-by-year fixed effects, which remove every state-wide shock in a year (state procurement, state reporting changes, statewide weather).

### Clustering and inference
- Default: standard errors clustered on the 2011 parent district (`shrug_key`, 605 clusters), heteroskedasticity-robust.
- Robustness: clustering on state (G = 24 to 30 depending on sample) with t(G-1) critical values, and the **restricted wild cluster bootstrap by state, Webb weights, 9,999 draws** for the irrigation page (scripts 124 and 130) and 999 draws in the neighbour and peer-effect referees (100, 132). Wild p-values move a little between runs (the tank term appears as 0.008, 0.009 and 0.010 in three outputs).
- Pairs (peer effects): dyadic-robust variance (Cameron and Miller 2014), run with members = 2011 units and with members = states.
- Other: cluster bootstrap on parents for balanced-panel changes (1,000 draws in 104, 2,000 in 108 and 109), bootstrap over states for the Measurement page's thresholded counts (28 states), randomisation inference for the Odisha Millets Mission (499 reshuffles over 30 districts), permutation of whole rainfall years (300), Moran's I with 999 permutations, Benjamini-Hochberg (BH) q-values within the stated family.

### Windows
- **Full period**: 1998-99 to 2019-20 (site default; district needs at least 10 years).
- **Recent windows** (irrigation v2): (a) 2015-16 to 2019-20; (b) 2017-18 to 2021-22 (flagged state-years dropped, six crops removed); extension-only 2020-21 and 2021-22 (district needs both years). A district needs 3 of 5 years in a window.
- **Early/late comparison windows**: 1998-99 to 2004-05 against 2013-14 to 2019-20 (Overview and D0 window changes); 1998-99 to 2000-01 against 2017-18 to 2019-20 (crop-group endpoint trends); 1998-99 to 2002-03 against 2015-16 to 2019-20 (peer effects, irrigation long differences); 2009-10 to 2013-14 (census-source comparison).
- Irrigation share = net irrigated / net sown = irr / (irr + unirr) in Antyodaya; script 72 corrected an earlier gross-cropped-area denominator.

## 5. Open questions and ideas not yet done

1. **Duflo-Pande river-gradient design for irrigation.** Not feasible on disk: SHRUG has elevation and ruggedness but no river network, no dam locations, no basin gradient. Plan recorded in `irrigation_results.md` section 5a: SRTM 90 m or HydroSHEDS conditioned DEM for slope; HydroRIVERS or WRIS rivers of order 3 or more; district shares of river-adjacent area in slope classes 0 to 1.5, 1.5 to 3, 3 to 6 and above 6 percent; CWC National Register of Large Dams (completion year, location, purpose) to build upstream and own-district dam counts by year; instrument = gradient shares x state dam count, with state x year FE. Check the first stage first, because post-1998 completions are fewer than in the original paper's period.
2. **A usable groundwater or pump-cost instrument.** CGWB data start in 2013 and are partly an outcome of the pumping. Needed: pre-period depth (CGWB yearbooks or India-WRIS station series from the 1990s) or a fixed hydrogeology layer (CGWB/NAQUIM principal aquifer type, alluvial against hard rock), interacted with a time-varying pump-cost shifter (state feeder separation dates such as Gujarat Jyotigram 2003-06, farm tariff or free-power episodes, national diesel price).
3. **State reporting differences as a confound for border effects.** APY is compiled by state departments, so a reporting practice that differs by state looks like a border effect. Evidence already in hand: adjacent districts across a state line differ by about five more crops in how long a list they report than same-state neighbours; dropping minor crops and the "other" group moves the likeness gap by less than a point (`Neighbours` page, `referee/h2_border_by_measure.csv`); Tamil Nadu reports rice under one season until 2016-17; Odisha's list jumps from 14 to 39 names in 2019-20; Madhya Pradesh named 17 crops in 1998-99. Not done: an independent source for crop area at the border (remote-sensing crop maps, or the ICRISAT district database), a test restricted to border pairs whose states share a reporting regime, and a reporting-regime break date for each state used as a pair-year control.
4. **Spread tests with power.** The year-on-year lag-lead placebo cannot discriminate (simulated b = 0.07 gives lag 0.062 and mirrored lead 0.056). The episode hazard has intervals of plus or minus 0.04 to 0.12 against base rates under 1.5 percent a year. Needs finer units (blocks or villages), or an event with an exogenous start (a seed release date, a plant opening).
5. **Treatment data for the policy event studies.** Missing: the 10 Assam and 15 Jharkhand NFSM-pulses districts of 2010-11, 2 Jharkhand rice districts, the 265-district coarse-cereal list for 2015-16 to 2017-18, A3P (2010-11) block lists, exact year the 199/119/557 lists became 194/126/638. Lok Sabha answers (eparlib.nic.in) did not resolve from this machine. OMM needs a valid control group (other states' millet programmes, or block-level data) and a fixed crop basket.
6. **Markets through time.** Facilities are seen once (Mission Antyodaya 2019-20) and set against 22 years of cropping, so every market result is a between-district association. Ideas, unchecked: build a 2011 baseline of market and haat availability from the Census village directory to separate older from newer facilities; Economic Census 2013 as a second wave for establishments. No referee pass has been run on the markets block beyond the irrigation v2 re-runs (state clustering on four headline rows, the population-weight reproduction).
7. **Irrigation panel coverage and shared reporting noise.** West Bengal (4 percent) and Maharashtra (21 percent) are almost absent from the LUS panel, and LUS and APY are both state agricultural statistics, so errors can travel together (the lag-one-year weakening in B13). An irrigation series from another office (ICRISAT DLD, state abstracts, Minor Irrigation Census) was judged unnecessary for the cross-section but would test this.
8. **Extension past 2019-20.** DES stops at 2022-23. The crop list breaks in 2020-21 (six crops removed, 14 state-years dropped; Telangana 137 to 152 percent of baseline area, Assam, Tamil Nadu and Karnataka crop cells above 1.5x baseline are kept). A stable state-specific crop list (variant `stable` in script 122) exists only for window (b). Telangana's 2019-20 rice jump (D6) could be re-examined with 2020-23 once Telangana's flags are resolved.
9. **Rebuild the deliverables.** The deck (`deck/crop_diversity.pptx`, 15 slides), the two notebooks, the master workbook and the regression Word document were built in September on the pre-revision basis (1997-98 inside the averages, inverted U as the irrigation headline, pooled-count slides). Their numbers are stale (section 6). **Done 7 Nov 2026** for the deck, workbook and results document; the notebooks carry superseded notes (section 6 item 5).
10. **Pulses jump after 2015 and Madhya Pradesh wheat 2018-20.** The national pulses share is flat at 11 to 13 percent until 2015-16 then 15 to 16 percent (`trends_results.md`); MP wheat at 7.7 and 10.2 Mha in 2018-19 and 2019-20 matches the state's own production figure and is kept. Neither is explained.
